SGE: Structure-Guided Extraction

Faithful graph construction from statistical tables for GraphRAG, eliminating structural blindness through topology-aware constrained extraction.

Problem

GraphRAG systems suffer from structural blindness when processing CSV and tabular data. Existing LLM-based extraction pipelines treat table cells as free text, ignoring the relational topology embedded in row/column structure — header hierarchies, merged cells, and cross-column constraints. This leads to hallucinated edges, broken entity references, and low-fidelity knowledge graphs that degrade downstream graph-native tasks. We formalize this as the Format-Constraint Coupling (FCC) problem: the structural format of a table implicitly constrains which entity-relation triples are semantically valid.

Approach

SGE introduces a 3-stage framework that makes table structure a first-class citizen in the extraction pipeline:

  1. Topology Recognition — Detect structural primitives: header depth, spanning cells, pivot axes, and relational keys. This stage produces a typed schema skeleton before any NLP extraction begins.
  2. Schema Induction — Infer a domain-specific entity-relation schema from the recognized topology, mapping column roles to graph node/edge types without relying on free-form prompting.
  3. Constrained Extraction — Execute LLM extraction under hard structural constraints derived from stages 1 and 2, preventing out-of-schema triples and enforcing referential integrity across the resulting graph.

The framework is system-agnostic and was validated on both LightRAG and MS GraphRAG backends using Claude Haiku and GPT-4o-mini as extraction models. We release CSVFidelity-Bench: 10 datasets across 3 domains (global health, economic development, clinical records) totaling 977 gold-standard facts.

Results

Dataset SGE FC Baseline FC Improvement
WHO Health Statistics 1.000 0.167 6.0×
World Bank Country Metadata 1.000 0.473 2.1×

SGE achieves valid improvements in all 9/9 comparable dataset-model pairs. On graph-native task accuracy, SGE scores 42/45 versus baseline 1/45 — a 42× improvement in answerable queries.

Role

  • Identified and formalized the Format-Constraint Coupling mechanism as the root cause of GraphRAG fidelity failures on tabular data.
  • Designed the 3-stage SGE framework and implemented it across LightRAG and MS GraphRAG backends.
  • Constructed CSVFidelity-Bench with 977 expert-annotated gold-standard facts across 10 datasets and 3 domains.
  • Conducted cross-system validation experiments with Claude Haiku and GPT-4o-mini, targeting EMNLP 2026 submission.
