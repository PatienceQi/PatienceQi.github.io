AI Customer Service Automation

Automating repetitive inquiries with guardrails and clear handoff to human agents.

Problem

Support volume surged while QA teams struggled to track policy compliance. The organization needed automation for common intents without compromising auditability or customer satisfaction.

Approach

I architected an Intent → Orchestration → Response → Feedback loop powered by Dify agents and custom monitoring dashboards. Each flow node carries risk thresholds that trigger human fallback when exceeded.

Results

Metric Before After
Automation coverage 18% 60%
CSAT 72% 90%

Role

  • Designed the intent classifier and cold-start data labeling playbook.
  • Built the orchestration engine covering 30+ scenarios with custom fallbacks.
  • Developed live dashboards with routing policies and alerting.
