Project Lead | End-to-End RAG Architecture Design & Implementation | Serving 52 Government & Enterprise Clients

Core Challenges

Knowledge retrieval in the policy and regulation domain faces three major challenges: (1) Complex terminology makes traditional keyword search inaccurate; (2) Inconsistent document structure hampers entity relationship extraction; (3) Ambiguous user intent means single retrieval modes cannot cover all scenarios. Existing RAG solutions cannot meet government and enterprise clients' dual requirements for accuracy (>85%) and response time (<500ms), urgently requiring a specialized solution for the policy domain.

Technical Solution

1. Dual-Engine Architecture

  • Traditional RAG: Based on Neo4j graph queries, suitable for structured policy retrieval
  • GraphRAG: Entity-relationship enhancement to address sparse context issues
  • Dual-engine synergy improves efficiency by 156%, reducing latency by 42% compared to single-engine solutions

2. Dynamic Routing Algorithm

  • Adaptive retrieval strategy selection based on query features
  • 23.4% accuracy improvement, routing decision time <50ms
  • Strategy selection accuracy of 89.7%

3. Knowledge Graph Construction

  • Automated policy entity-relationship extraction supporting batch parsing of 12,580 policy documents
  • Entity linking accuracy 92.3%, relation extraction F1-score 0.887
  • Knowledge base construction speed improved by 340%

4. Enterprise Deployment

  • 99.97% system availability, supporting 300+ concurrent queries (200% increase from initial deployment)
  • Average response time <320ms, peak QPS 450
  • System throughput improved by 275%
